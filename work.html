EXECUTION SEQUENCE

How It Works

Every project follows the same core sequence. Clean, predictable, no surprises.

01

Briefing

You reach out with the basics — sport, date, location, and what you need the images for. I come back with a scope, shot list, and quote within 48 hours.

02

Pre-Production

I scout locations (in-person or virtually), lock in credentials and access if needed, and finalize the shot plan. You approve, we're locked.

03

Capture

Game day. I show up early, position for the key moments, and shoot continuously.

04

Post & Delivery

Cull, color grade, and export. You get a private gallery link with download access. Same-day highlights available for social teams on tight deadlines.
